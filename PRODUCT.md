# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Creative developers, musicians, interactive designers, and portfolio visitors exploring browser-based music interaction. They use the app in a desktop or laptop browser with a webcam, often as a live demo of hand tracking, Web Audio, and reactive visuals.

## Product Purpose

Hand Music Controller turns webcam hand gestures into an expressive electronic music instrument. Success means a visitor understands the interaction within seconds, can start the camera/audio flow confidently, and sees immediate sound plus visual feedback from their hands.

## Brand Personality

Futuristic, musical, precise. The product should feel like a polished dark music lab: energetic enough for rave/techno, but clear and controlled enough to trust as an interactive instrument.

## Anti-references

Do not make it feel like a raw technical experiment, a generic SaaS dashboard, a toy for children, a flat empty dark page, or an overdone cyberpunk interface with illegible neon. Avoid visual clutter that competes with the actual hand-controlled instrument.

## Design Principles

- Make the instrument understandable within five seconds.
- Put immediate feedback near every action: camera, audio, hands, gestures, and visuals.
- Preserve the live performance feeling: sound and visuals should remain the main event.
- Use futuristic polish in service of clarity, not decoration.
- Keep the camera/privacy flow transparent and reassuring.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target readable contrast, keyboard-focusable controls, semantic structure, and reduced-motion alternatives. The app should not rely on color alone for state. Camera runs locally in the browser, and the UI should state that no video is uploaded.
